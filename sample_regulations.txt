NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE
ACADEMIC REGULATIONS AND GUIDELINES (2025-2026)

Section 1: Attendance Requirements
1.1 Minimum Attendance: Every registered student must secure a minimum aggregate attendance of 75% across all lecture and practical sessions in a semester to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
1.2 Medical Exemption: Students with documented medical emergencies may be granted condonation up to 10% (lowering the threshold to 65%) upon submission of verified hospital records to the Dean of Academic Affairs within 5 working days of resumption.
1.3 Shortage of Attendance: Any student having less than 65% attendance under any circumstances will be detained and required to re-register for the course in the subsequent academic year.

Section 2: Examination and Grading Policy
2.1 Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 40% of the total marks are allocated to continuous assessment comprising two cycle tests (15% each) and assignments/quizzes (10%).
2.2 End Semester Examination: The final written examination constitutes 60% of the course weightage.
2.3 Passing Minimum: A candidate must secure at least 45% in the end semester examination and a cumulative aggregate of 50% across both CIA and final exam to earn course credits.
2.4 Grade System: Letter grades range from O (Outstanding, 90-100 marks, Grade Point 10), A+ (80-89 marks, Grade Point 9), down to F (Fail, below 50 marks, Grade Point 0).

Section 3: Library Rules and Borrowing Limits
3.1 Borrowing Privileges: Undergraduate students are eligible to borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for a maximum duration of 14 days. Postgraduate scholars may borrow up to 6 books for 21 days.
3.2 Late Fines: Overdue books will incur a fine of 5 rupees per day per book for the first week, escalating to 10 rupees per day thereafter.
3.3 Lost Books: If an institutional library book is lost, the borrower must replace it with an identical or newer edition of the book, plus pay an administrative processing fee of 200 rupees.

Section 4: Anti-Ragging Policy and Code of Conduct
4.1 Zero Tolerance: Ragging in any form inside or outside the campus premises is strictly prohibited and constitutes a cognizable criminal offense.
4.2 Disciplinary Actions: Any student found guilty of ragging will face immediate suspension, rustication from hostel facilities, and potential expulsion following an inquiry by the Proctorial Board.
